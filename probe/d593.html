About Magnus Steel & Infra Ltd.

Incorporated in February 1978, Magnus Steel & Infra Limited is stated business activities include the trading, import and export of iron and steel products, ferroalloys and other related industrial materials. Its business objectives also cover dealing in ferrous and non-ferrous metals, including bars, rods, sheets, plates, strips and other steel products. The Company’s scope of activities includes operating as a dealer, stockist, wholesaler, retailer, agent, broker, importer and exporter of these products.

According to its FY2024–25 annual report, the Company resumed trading in iron and steel products, including engineering products, during the financial year. Revenue from operations increased from ₹66.54 lakh in FY2024 to ₹318.81 lakh in FY2025. The Company reported a profit after tax of ₹5.67 lakh in FY2025, compared with a loss of ₹37.81 lakh in FY2024.

The Company’s registered office is located in Shirasgaon, Niphad, Nashik, Maharashtra. While its current corporate name reflects a focus on the steel and infrastructure sector, its latest available FY2024–25 financial disclosures describe trading in iron and steel products as its resumed business activity.
