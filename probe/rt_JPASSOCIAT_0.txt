Rationale and key rating drivers

resolution plan (RP) approved by National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT). In line with the RP implemented, the company has
repaid/extinguished the aforementioned bank facilities/instruments and there is no amount outstanding under the facilities and
instruments as on date vide the No Dues Certificates from trustees/lenders and charge satisfaction on Ministry of Corporate
