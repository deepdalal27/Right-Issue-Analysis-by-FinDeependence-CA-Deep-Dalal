About Kanel Industries Ltd.

Kanel Industries Ltd. was incorporated in 1992 and is engaged in the trading of edible oils, including castor oil, cottonseed oil, groundnut oil, sunflower oil, palmolein oil and soybean oil.

The company’s product portfolio also includes various grades of castor oil, such as refined castor oil, premium refined castor oil and pharmaceutical-grade castor oil, along with castor meal.

Its registered office is located in Ahmedabad, Gujarat
